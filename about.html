About Segecha Group Ltd.

Your trusted partner in long-haul transportation across East Africa.

Our story

Segecha Group Ltd. is a leading provider of long-haul freight and logistics in East Africa. We've built our reputation on reliability, efficiency, and customer focus.

Our modern fleet, tracking technology, and experienced drivers ensure your cargo is delivered safely and on time. We understand the region’s challenges and deliver solutions that work.

We combine technology with local expertise to serve businesses across Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda and Burundi.

Why East Africa

East Africa’s trade corridors — Mombasa to Kampala, Dar to Kigali, and the Northern Corridor — connect ports to landlocked markets. Border procedures and regulations vary by country. We know these routes and work with customs and partners so your cargo moves smoothly and on time, without surprises.
